FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, process, and how we can help your business grow.

What services does Arthasetu Fin Hub offer?

We offer Corporate Debt Syndication, MSME Debt Syndication, Bill Discounting Limits Syndication, Credit Rating Advisory, Pre-IPO Advisory, Project Advisory, and Mergers & Acquisitions advisory services.

How can I schedule a consultation?

You can fill out the contact form above, call us directly at +91 91212 27666, or email us at director@arthasetu.com. Our team will get back to you within 24 hours.

Do you work with businesses outside Hyderabad?

Yes, while our office is in Hyderabad, we serve clients across India. We offer both in-person and virtual consultation options to accommodate businesses nationwide.

What industries do you specialize in?

We work across manufacturing sectors (Aerospace, Automotive, Pharma, Textiles, etc.) and service sectors (Healthcare, Education, IT, Infrastructure, Real Estate, and more).

What is the typical turnaround time for debt syndication?

Turnaround time varies depending on deal complexity and documentation readiness. Typically, MSME syndication takes 4–6 weeks, while corporate debt syndication may take 6–12 weeks.

Is there a fee for the initial consultation?

We offer a complimentary initial consultation to understand your requirements and recommend the best approach. Detailed engagement terms are shared after the initial discussion.
