Asset Leasing & Equipment Finance Advisory

Service Overview

Asset Leasing & Equipment Finance Advisory enables businesses to acquire capital assets - such as manufacturing machinery, IT equipment, commercial vehicles, and infrastructure assets - without tying up operational cash flow. At Arthasetu, we structure customized leasing models (Operating and Financial) that optimize balance sheet ratios, provide tax benefits, and preserve working capital.

What the Service Includes:

  • Finance Lease & Operating Lease Structuring

    Structuring lease terms that align with the asset's useful life and the company's financial objectives.

  • Equipment & Machinery Debt Syndication

    Securing structured term loans specifically for high-value industrial machinery procurement.

  • Technology & IT Asset Leasing

    Facilitating leases for IT infrastructure, software licenses, and office automation tools with periodic upgrade options.

  • Commercial Vehicle & Fleet Finance

    Structuring leasing programs for transport operators, logistics companies, and corporate fleets.

  • Sale & Leaseback Transactions

    Assisting companies in unlocking capital from existing assets by selling them to a lessor and leasing them back.

  • Tax & Balance Sheet Advisory

    Advising on the accounting treatment, depreciation benefits, and tax deductions associated with leasing.

Benefits of Asset Leasing & Equipment Finance Advisory

Capital Preservation

Acquire critical business assets without large initial cash outflows, keeping capital free for core growth.

Balance Sheet Optimization

Off-balance-sheet financing options that improve debt-to-equity ratios and return on assets.

Tax Efficiency & Deductions

Lease rental payments are often fully tax-deductible as operating expenses, reducing net tax liabilities.

Technology Obsolescence Protection

Operating leases allow easy upgrades to newer technology at the end of the lease term.

Why Choose Our Asset Leasing & Equipment Finance Advisory

  • Customized Lease Structuring

    Designing leases tailored to asset depreciation, cash flow seasonality, and project duration.

  • Extensive Lessor Network

    Relationships with domestic and international leasing companies, specialized funds, and banks.

  • Industry-Specific Knowledge

    Understanding the residual value and lifecycle dynamics of diverse equipment and manufacturing machinery.

  • End-to-End Asset Sourcing Support

    Advising on procurement terms, import regulations, and equipment vendor coordination.

  • Transparent Terms

    Ensuring no hidden termination charges, maintenance clauses, or complex end-of-lease buy-out terms.
