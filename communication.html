Strategic Financial Advisory for the Communication Sector

Communication businesses invest in infrastructure years before subscriber revenue catches up and the financing structures that ignore this gap create servicing pressure that slows network growth. We help telecom operators, ISPs, and tower businesses access capital structured around how communication businesses actually build and generate revenue.

Our Solutions

Comprehensive Financial Services for Communication

Network Infrastructure Financing

We structure long-tenure term loans for tower construction, fibre rollout , data center development, and last-mile conncetivity projects- with repayment schedules built around subscriber revenue ramp-up, not standard EMI patterns that create servicing pressure before the network is fully utilised.

Spectrum and Licensing Debt Structing

Specturm payment obligations create large, front-loaded liabilites that must be serviced from revenue that buildsgradually. We structure debt facilities that accounts for defferred payment schedules, licence renewal timelines, and revenue ramp-up so spectrum investment does not compromise opreational cash flow.

Working Capital for Network Opreations

Running a communication network involes significant recurring costs- maintenance ,power,lease obligations, and customer acquisition that must be managed consistently regardless of subscriber payment cycles. We structure working capital facilities that keep opreations funded without creating unesscessary short-term debt exposure.

Our Approach

How We Support Communication Sector Financing

Communication infrastructure is not a standard asset class and its financing should not be treated as one. subscriber growth curves, specturm obligation, ARPU trends, and long asset payback periods all shape how capital should be structed. Our approach starts with unerstanding your networks stage, licensing position, and revenue model before we indentify the right lenders and structures.

01

Network and Revenue Model Assessment

We analyse your subscriber growth projections, ARPU trends, infrastructure rollout plan, and spectrum reflects realistic revenue ramp-up and debt service capicity across the network lifecycle.

02

Lender Identification and Positioning

We identify banks, NBFCs, and development finance institutions with genuine appetite for comunication sector expourse and present your business against the metrics they actually use to asses telecom and ISP businesses, not standard industrial credit norms.

03

Documentation and Funding Closure

We manage end-to end documentation, from DPR preparation and CMA data to regulatory compliance dcoumentation and lender coordination, ensuring nothing delays disbursment once credit approval is in place.
