Service / Project Advisory

Project Advisory

Service Overview

Behind every major infrastructure project, industrial plant, or real estate development is a financing challenge that is far more complex than a standard business loan. Project financing requires a fundamentally different structure one where repayment is tied to project cash flows, multiple lenders may need to be coordinated, and the detailed project report must meet the standards of institutional lenders and government bodies.
At Arthasetu Fin Hub, Project Advisory is designed to help businesses, developers, and entrepreneurs secure structured project financing with the right preparation and lender approach. From feasibility analysis and DPR preparation to lender identification and funding coordination every aspect of the project financing process is managed with domain expertise and execution focus.
Whether you are setting up a manufacturing facility, developing an infrastructure asset, expanding an existing plant, or exploring government-backed project funding the advisory ensures your project is presented to lenders in the most credible, well-structured form possible.

What the Service Includes:

  • Project Feasibility & Financial Viability Assessment

    An in-depth evaluation of project economics, cost structure, revenue projections, IRR, DSCR, and financial viability establishing whether the project is fundable and identifying the right financing approach.

  • Detailed Project Report (DPR) Preparation

    Preparation of a comprehensive, lender-ready Detailed Project Report covering project scope, technical parameters, cost estimates, financial projections, and risk factors structured to meet the expectations of banks, DFIs, and government funding bodies.

  • Project Financing Structuring

    Strategic advisory on optimal debt-equity mix, loan tenure, moratorium period, security structuring, and repayment design aligned with project cash flows and lender requirements.

  • Government & Institutional Funding Coordination

    Identification of and coordination with Development Finance Institutions (DFIs), NABARD, SIDBI, state government schemes, and central government programmes relevant to your project sector and scale.

  • Lender & Investor Identification

    Coordinating with banks, NBFCs, infrastructure funds, and institutional investors to identify the most suitable funding partners for the project based on size, sector, and financial structure.

  • Regulatory Clearance & Compliance Advisory

    Guidance on regulatory approvals, environmental clearances, sector-specific licensing requirements, and compliance documentation that lenders and government bodies require before sanctioning project funds.

Benefits of Project Advisory

A Fundable Project from Day One

Proper financial structuring, accurate DPR preparation, and a well-documented financing plan significantly improve lender confidence and reduce the back-and-forth that delays project funding decisions.

Access to the Right Funding Channels

Different projects attract different lenders and funding programmes. Project advisory ensures your project reaches the most appropriate lenders whether that is a consortium of banks, a DFI, or a government scheme.

Stronger Project Credibility

A professionally prepared DPR backed by credible financial projections and structured advisory positions your project as serious and well-planned building trust with lenders, investors, and government stakeholders.

Faster Evaluation and Approval Timelines

Projects that arrive at lenders with complete, accurate, and well-organized documentation move through credit assessment significantly faster than those that require multiple rounds of revision and resubmission.
